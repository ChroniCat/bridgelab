How are metric alerts qualified?

Use the same lens, resolution, section reference and threshold. Capture at least 10 usable calibration trials including three zero runs, three above threshold and two distinct nonzero amplitudes. Local scale and foreshortening define the view groups; the error estimate is frozen before holdout.

For each view group, hold out at least three zero runs and three above threshold. Qualification requires no false alert or miss, 70% decision-time coverage and an empirical error below the threshold. Vary lighting, occlusion and camera movement as in actual use. Empirical P95 takes the 95th percentile of valid sample errors within each trial, then the 95th percentile across trials. The observed performance applies to the tested conditions.
